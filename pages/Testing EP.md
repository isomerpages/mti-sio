<h1>Advisory on Managing Severe Haze Conditions</h1>
<p>During periods of severe haze, companies should implement the following
measures to protect employees' health and maintain business continuity.</p>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/pic2.jpg">
</div>
<h4>Health Protection Measures</h4>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Provide N95 masks to employees who need to work outdoors or whose work
requires frequent outdoor exposure</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Set up air purifiers in office spaces and ensure regular maintenance of
air filtration systems</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Monitor PSI levels regularly through official NEA channels and communicate
updates to staff</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Consider allowing employees with pre-existing respiratory or heart conditions
to work from home when PSI reaches unhealthy levels</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h4>Workplace Arrangements</h4>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Implement flexible work arrangements including remote working options
when PSI reaches hazardous levels</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Minimise outdoor work activities and reschedule non-essential outdoor
events</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Ensure indoor areas are well-sealed to prevent haze from entering</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Keep windows closed and use air-conditioning in recirculation mode</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h4>Business Continuity Plans</h4>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Review and activate business continuity plans if severe haze affects normal
operations</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Ensure critical operations can continue with minimal disruption</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Maintain clear communication channels with employees, customers, and stakeholders</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Keep essential supplies such as N95 masks and air purifiers adequately
stocked</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Companies should stay updated on official advisories from relevant government
agencies and adjust their measures accordingly based on haze severity levels.</p>
